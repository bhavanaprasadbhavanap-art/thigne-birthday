Dear Thigne 🤥,

My favourite person

Okay… you finally reached the last page. 😂
Honestly, I’m surprised you had this much patience.

I wanted to leave this letter for the very end because there are some things I probably wouldn’t say properly if I was just sitting in front of you.

First of all… Happy Birthday, idiot. 🤥❤️

I still don’t understand how someone can be this annoying and still become such an important person in my life. Seriously, there should be some award for that. 😂

We’ve had so many random conversations, stupid arguments, unnecessary teasing, laughing for no reason, and those moments where one of us says something completely normal and somehow we both end up laughing like idiots.

And honestly, those are the moments I’ll remember the most.

Not the perfect moments.
The random ones.
The silly ones.
The moments that probably meant nothing to anyone else, but somehow meant a lot to me.

Sometimes I think about how easily friendships start. One day you’re just talking normally, and before you realize it, that person becomes someone you look for when something happens.

Someone you want to tell things to.
Someone whose absence you actually notice.

And somehow, you became that person.

I know we’ve had our misunderstandings too. We’ve annoyed each other, irritated each other, and probably tested each other’s patience more times than necessary. 😂

But I think that’s also what makes a friendship real.

It isn't always perfect.
Sometimes we don't understand each other.
Sometimes we say things we don't mean.
Sometimes we get busy and talk less.

And maybe, as life goes on, things will change.
That part honestly makes me a little sad.

Because I don't know where we'll both be a few years from now.
Maybe we'll be busy with our careers.
Maybe we'll make new friends.
Maybe we'll talk every day.
Maybe we'll barely talk at all.
I don't know.

But I really hope that no matter how much life changes, you never look back at this friendship and think it meant nothing.

Because to me, it didn't.

You became a small but very special part of my life, and I'm genuinely thankful for that.

Thank you for all the laughs.
Thank you for all the stupid moments.
Thank you for listening to me, even when I probably made absolutely no sense. 😂

And thank you for simply being there and being you.

I don't need our friendship to be perfect.
I just hope it stays real.

And if someday we're sitting somewhere years from now, remembering all this, I hope we'll laugh and say,

“How stupid we were.” 😂

Because honestly… we probably were.

But those stupid days were also some of the happiest ones.

So, Thigne 🤥…

I hope you get everything you're working for.
I hope you become the person you want to become.
I hope life gives you more reasons to smile than to worry.

And if life ever gets difficult, I hope you remember that there was once a girl who was ridiculously proud to call you her friend.

Maybe I don't say it often.
Maybe I hide it behind jokes and teasing.
But you really do matter to me.

Happy Birthday, Thigne 🤥❤️

And thank you…
For being a part of my story.

I don't know what the future looks like.
But whatever happens, I'll always be grateful that our paths crossed.

— Bhavana ❤️
